# Audio credits

The sea, wind, rain and thunder sounds are synthesized at runtime (`src/audio/audio.js`,
`src/audio/synth.js`), except the four recorded distant thunder rolls below. They are excerpts
(trimmed, faded, loudness-normalized, re-encoded as MP3) of an openly licensed recording from
Wikimedia Commons and are shared under the same license as their source.

| File | Source recording | Author | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `thunder_a.mp3` – `thunder_d.mp3` | [Rhumphries - rbh-thunder-storm.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rhumphries_-_rbh-thunder-storm.ogg) | RHumphries | [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) |

Every new sound must meet the licensing standard in [docs/licenses.md](../../docs/licenses.md)
(CC0, public domain, CC BY or our own; no NC, SA or ND) and be registered there.

## Former recordings (removed; credited only for the film's soundtrack)

These CC BY-SA 4.0 recordings were removed in October 2026 because a commercial release cannot
carry the share-alike condition; their sounds are now synthesized. The film
`media/open-water.mp4` was rendered before that and its soundtrack still contains excerpts of
them, so the film's audio is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 and must not be used commercially.
